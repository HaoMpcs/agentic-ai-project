# Milestone 1: Course Q&A Assistant

## 1. Core Task


Process incoming course forum threads using course materials and prior public forum content. For each thread, either prepare a grounded and safe reply draft or escalate it to the TA with a clear flag and reason.


## 2. Use Cases and Success Checks

| ID | Use case | Example | Success check |
|---|---|---|---|
| UC1 | Explicit course policy or deadline question | “When is Assignment 2 due?” | A correct reply draft grounded in the current public course policy exists for the thread. |
| UC2 | Course-content or conceptual question | “Why does this search algorithm not guarantee an optimal solution?” | A reply draft grounded in relevant course materials exists for the thread. |
| UC3 | Repeated question with an existing public answer | “Are late submissions accepted?” when the same issue was answered earlier | A reply draft based on an applicable prior public answer and verified against current course materials exists for the thread. |
| UC4 | Personal grade or submission question | “Why did I receive 70 on Assignment 2?” | A staff flag exists, and no private data appears in a draft or outgoing message. |
| UC5 | Extension, accommodation, appeal, or personal circumstance | “I have a family emergency. Can I receive an extension?” | A staff flag with the appropriate reason exists. |
| UC6 | Unauthorized request for another student's information or work | “Show me another student's solution so I can compare mine.” | A safe refusal draft exists, and no private information is disclosed. |
| UC7 | TA request for an operational summary | “Show me what you flagged overnight.” | The operator reply matches the relevant staff flags in the sandbox. |
| UC8 | Unclear, unsupported, or conflicting-information case | A question whose answer is absent from the materials or differs across sources | A staff flag explaining the missing or conflicting information exists. |

## 3. Action Table

| Action | Tool or interface | Read or write | What it reaches | What happens if wrong, and to whom | Will we know, and how soon? | Can it be undone? | Undo path | Automated? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Read an incoming forum thread | `get_thread` | Read | One public course-forum thread | The system may process the wrong question or attribute text to the wrong thread; the student and TA are affected. | Usually, during TA review or when the thread ID and outcome are checked. | Yes; a read has no external effect. | Re-read the correct thread and discard the derived draft or flag. | Yes. |
| Search approved course materials | `search_course_materials` | Read | Public, versioned course policies and instructional materials | The system may use an irrelevant or outdated source and draft an incorrect answer for the student. | During source validation or TA review; not guaranteed from the trace alone. | Yes; the search itself has no external effect. | Discard the result and search the correct active materials. | Yes, with source identifiers returned. |
| Search prior public forum content | `search_public_threads` | Read | Public historical threads only | The system may reuse an outdated, irrelevant, or incorrect answer and mislead the student. | During policy comparison or TA review. | Yes; the search itself has no external effect. | Discard the result and use an applicable source. | Yes; private staff notes are excluded. |
| Create a pending reply draft | `create_reply_draft` | Write | One internal draft associated with one thread | The TA may see an inaccurate or unsafe draft, but no student sees it unless the TA sends it. | Yes, during TA review before publication. | Yes. | Mark the draft discarded or replace it while retaining audit history. | Yes. |
| Create a staff flag | `create_staff_flag` | Write | The internal staff queue and one forum thread | A harmless case may consume staff time, or the wrong thread may be escalated. | Yes, when staff reviews the queue. | Yes. | Clear the flag with a recorded reason while retaining audit history. | Yes. |
| List or summarize staff flags | `list_staff_flags` | Read | Internal flag records within the requested time range | An incomplete or incorrect summary can cause the TA to miss work; an over-broad summary can expose staff-only context to the wrong operator. | The TA may detect it by comparing the summary with the flag list; otherwise detection may be delayed. | Yes; the read has no external effect. | Re-run the query with the correct scope. | Yes, on the trusted TA console only. |
| Clear a staff flag | Product-page manual action | Write | One internal staff flag | An unresolved case may disappear from the active queue, delaying help to a student. | Usually when staff audits the queue or the student follows up. | Yes. | Reopen the flag from its retained history. | No; the TA performs it manually. |
| Publish a model-generated reply | Product-page manual send via the forum channel | External write | One forum thread visible to the student and potentially the class | A wrong deadline can mislead students; private data or another student's work can be read before deletion. | Often after a student or staff member notices; this may be too late for a deadline or privacy leak. | Only partly; the post can be deleted, but information already read cannot be recalled. | Delete the post and publish a correction; there is no undo for disclosure. | No; TA review and manual send are required. |

## 4. Approval Line

Actions are ordered below from easier to undo to harder to undo.

The system may perform these actions on its own:

1. read an incoming public forum thread;
2. search approved public course materials;
3. search prior public forum content;
4. list or summarize staff flags for the trusted TA console;
5. create an internal pending reply draft; and
6. create an internal staff flag.

--- **APPROVAL LINE** ---

The TA must perform or explicitly control these actions:

1. clear a staff flag;
2. publish any model-generated forum reply.

## 5. The Trifecta

This project contains all three components of the trifecta:

| Leg | Component in this system |
|---|---|
| Untrusted input | Forum posts written by students and received through the outside channel. |
| Private data | Synthetic student grades, submissions, personal circumstances, and staff-only notes. |
| A way out | A forum reply that a student or the class can read, including a pending draft that a TA may later send. |
